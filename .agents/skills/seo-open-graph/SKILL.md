name: seo-open-graph
description: Configure SEO meta tags, Open Graph, and WhatsApp/Social preview cards for landing pages.
---
When updating page metadata or preparing for launch:
1. Ensure `<title>` and `<meta name="description">` accurately reflect the current offer and dates.
2. Configure `og:title`, `og:description`, `og:url`, `og:type="website"`, and `og:image` (1200x630 WebP/JPG) plus `twitter:card="summary_large_image"`.
3. Verify all social preview image paths resolve properly in production.
